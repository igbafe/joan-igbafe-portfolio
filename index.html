01THE PERSON BEHIND
THE PAPERWORK

A clear desk makes room for good work

The courtroom gets the credit.
Your overflowing desk gets me.

The inbox, the files, the deadlines and the slightly nervous client get me. I’m Joan, a Virtual Legal Assistant who takes on the work around every matter: forms, filings, correspondence, calendars and follow-up.

When I handle it, your desk clears and the case gets room to breathe. Your clients notice, too, because the small, steady details shape how they remember your firm.

I came to legal work through a fascination with structure: the forms, templates and routines that tell a matter how to open, move and close. I also know that a client remembers how they were treated. I want an attorney to have room to work, and a client to have a good reason to come back.

Confidentiality is part of that care. I treat every email, file and deadline as part of the client experience and one less thing on your desk.

TRAINING PROJECT 01 · SCHUYLER & GRANGER LLP

E-filing tracker

A single, filterable record for each filing, from deadline to saved receipt.

What it tracks

Filing ID; client and matter; case number; practice area; document title; filing type; filing deadline; date filed; filing platform; person responsible; filing fee; and receipt status.

Why the structure helps

Three grouped areas—The Matter, The Document and The Submission—keep related details together. Dropdowns help keep entries uniform, while filters bring open items into view quickly.
